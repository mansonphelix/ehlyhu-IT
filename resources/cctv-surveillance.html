CCTV Surveillance Solutions Brief

End-to-end IP video surveillance solutions engineered for comprehensive visibility, security monitoring, and regulatory compliance across enterprise facilities.

Facility Survey & Coverage Planning

Every engagement begins with a detailed facility survey conducted by certified surveillance engineers. The survey assesses lighting conditions, structural obstacles, sight-line constraints, and regulatory requirements specific to your jurisdiction. A coverage plan is produced that maps camera placement, lens selection, and field of view to eliminate blind spots while respecting privacy obligations.

IP Camera Installation

We deploy PoE IP cameras with enterprise-grade ratings for weather, tampering, and low-light performance. Cameras are connected through managed switches with VLAN segregation that isolates video traffic from corporate data networks. Mounting hardware is selected for the specific surface and environmental conditions, and all external cabling is concealed or ruggedized for durability.

Storage Architecture

Network video recorders are sized according to resolution, frame rate, camera count, and retention period requirements. Redundant RAID storage protects against disk failure, and archival policies tier older footage to cost-optimized storage. Retention periods are configured to satisfy regulatory mandates and internal investigation requirements, with audit logs tracking all access to recorded footage.

Remote Access & Alerts

Authorized personnel access live and recorded video through encrypted web and mobile clients that support multi-factor authentication. Motion detection rules are tuned to reduce false alarms from environmental factors, and critical alerts are delivered through SMS, email, and push notification channels. Camera health monitoring automatically reports offline devices, storage failures, and performance degradation.

Compliance & Audit

Our systems are designed to support audit requirements common to regulated industries. Access logs, retention certificates, and chain-of-custody records are maintained for all footage. Integration with access control systems enables correlation between door events and video, supporting incident investigation and compliance reporting.
