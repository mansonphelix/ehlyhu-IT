Cloud Architecture · Migration Guide

Cloud & Hybrid Architecture Brief

Migrate to the cloud with confidence or optimize existing infrastructure. This brief covers roadmap design, cloud architecture, and ongoing governance across AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud.

Migration Roadmap Design

EHLYHU develops cloud migration roadmaps that sequence workloads by dependency, risk, and business value. Each wave is planned with defined entry and exit criteria, rollback procedures, and validation checkpoints. The roadmap accounts for data volume, integration complexity, and team readiness so that migration proceeds without disrupting production operations.

Multi-Cloud Architecture

We design architectures that leverage the strengths of multiple cloud platforms without creating operational fragmentation. AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud are evaluated against workload requirements, cost profiles, and service availability. Hybrid deployments connect cloud resources to on-premises infrastructure through encrypted, high-availability failover links that maintain performance for latency-sensitive applications.

High-Availability Failover

Critical workloads are deployed across availability zones with automated failover that triggers within seconds of an outage. Health checks monitor application responsiveness and traffic is rerouted to healthy regions through global load balancing. Recovery objectives are tested quarterly to ensure that failover behaves as designed under realistic failure scenarios.

FinOps Cost Management

Cloud spend can quickly exceed budget without disciplined governance. We implement tagging standards, spending alerts, and rightsizing recommendations that are reviewed monthly. Reserved capacity is purchased strategically for predictable workloads, and idle resources are automatically identified and remediated. Cost reports are delivered to leadership with business-unit-level attribution.

Identity & Access Governance

Cloud identity is integrated with your corporate directory through federated single sign-on. Access is governed by role-based policies with just-in-time elevation for administrative tasks. Multi-factor authentication is enforced for all privileged access, and access reviews are conducted quarterly to detect excessive permissions and orphaned accounts.

Ongoing Workload Monitoring

After migration, we provide continuous monitoring of performance, security posture, and cost. Dashboards surface utilization trends, anomaly detection, and compliance status. Our operations team reviews these metrics weekly and delivers optimization recommendations so that your cloud environment remains efficient and secure over time.
