Security Operations · Defense Brief

Cybersecurity & Resilience Brief

Threat surfaces evolve continuously. This brief outlines proactive multi-layered defenses protecting endpoints, email, identity stores, and corporate data across the enterprise.

Defense-in-Depth Architecture

EHLYHU implements layered security controls so that no single failure exposes the organization. Controls are applied at the network perimeter, endpoint, identity, data, and application layers. Each layer is monitored independently, and alerts are correlated across layers to reduce false positives and surface attack chains that span multiple control boundaries.

Vulnerability Management

Our vulnerability assessment program scans assets on a defined cadence and prioritizes findings based on exploitability, asset criticality, and exposure. Findings are tracked through remediation with defined ownership and target resolution dates. Monthly reporting provides leadership with a risk-prioritized view of the security posture that supports informed decision-making.

Endpoint Detection & Response

Every endpoint is instrumented with EDR technology that continuously monitors process behavior, registry changes, and network connections. The EDR platform uses behavioral heuristics to detect malware that signature-based tools miss. Our security operations center reviews EDR telemetry around the clock and initiates containment within the response window defined in your agreement.

Identity & Access Governance

Identity is the new perimeter. We enforce least-privilege access through role-based control, multi-factor authentication, and just-in-time elevation for administrative tasks. Access reviews are conducted quarterly to detect orphaned accounts and excessive privileges, and automated provisioning workflows reduce the risk of human error during onboarding and role changes.

Email & Phishing Defense

Our email security platform filters malicious messages at the gateway using content analysis, reputation checking, and behavioral heuristics. DMARC, DKIM, and SPF are enforced to prevent domain spoofing. Users participate in simulated phishing campaigns that measure susceptibility and deliver targeted training to improve detection capability over time.

Incident Response & Forensics

When an incident occurs, our response team assembles within the committed response window and assumes command of the engagement. Forensic evidence is collected, preserved, and analyzed to determine scope and impact. Containment actions are executed, eradication is verified, and a lessons-learned report is delivered with hardening recommendations to prevent recurrence.

Proactive defense reduces breach probability and regulatory exposure. Every endpoint is monitored 24/7 by a certified security operations center.
