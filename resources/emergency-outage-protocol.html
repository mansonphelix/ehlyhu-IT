Crisis Response · Security Officer Reference

Emergency Outage Protocol

Immediate-action reference sheet outlining crisis triage procedures, high-priority escalation contacts, and ransomware containment guidelines for security officers and operations leaders.

Immediate Triage Procedure

When a critical incident occurs, follow this sequence without deviation. First, isolate the affected system from the network to prevent lateral movement. Second, notify the EHLYHU 24/7 dispatch desk immediately via the hotline. Third, preserve evidence by capturing system logs, memory dumps, and network flow data before any remediation action is taken. Fourth, do not attempt ransom payment or unauthorized recovery until a senior incident commander has assessed the situation.

Escalation Contacts

EHLYHU maintains a dedicated emergency desk operating 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. All critical incidents bypass standard support queues and are routed directly to on-call senior engineers. The dispatch desk maintains direct line access to regional partner nodes for on-site intervention when remote remediation is insufficient.

Ransomware Containment

Ransomware requires immediate isolation and careful handling. Disconnect affected endpoints from all network segments including wireless and VPN connections. Disable shared drives and cloud sync clients to prevent encryption propagation. Preserve the original encrypted state for forensic analysis. Notify law enforcement through the appropriate channel in coordination with our incident commander. Do not pay the ransom — our recovery model relies on air-gapped backups and verified restoration procedures that do not depend on attacker cooperation.

Post-Incident Review

Every critical incident triggers a formal post-incident review conducted jointly by EHLYHU and your security leadership. The review documents the attack vector, containment effectiveness, recovery time against agreed targets, and recommended hardening measures. A remediation roadmap is delivered within 48 hours of incident resolution.
