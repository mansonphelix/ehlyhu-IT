Full-Spectrum IT Services Brief

Every IT challenge. One global solution. This brief catalogs the complete range of technology services EHLYHU delivers across hardware, software, networking, cloud, security, surveillance, backup, and strategic advisory.

Why a Single-Source Partner Matters

Enterprises that manage IT through multiple disjointed vendors experience longer resolution times, duplicated administrative effort, and unclear accountability during incidents. EHLYHU operates as one accountable partner responsible for the entire technology stack. One contract, one support desk, one escalation path, and one named operations lead own the outcome from desktop to data center to cloud.

Hardware Support & Lifecycle

We restore, upgrade, and manage physical technology assets with engineering precision. Services cover desktop, laptop, workstation, and server diagnostics and repair, component-level board and cooling recovery, lifecycle provisioning, and on-site parts dispatch coordinated through a single logistics desk.

Software & Application Excellence

Software friction degrades productivity. We resolve operating system faults, productivity suite deployments, collaboration tool configuration, and license compliance issues with permanent solutions. Support covers Windows, macOS, and Linux enterprise environments with hardened deployment practices.

Networking & Infrastructure

Reliable connectivity is mission critical. We design, deploy, and monitor high-speed secure networks that maintain throughput under peak enterprise loads. Capabilities include enterprise LAN and WAN design, managed router and switch configuration, Zero-Trust Network Access, and secure site-to-site VPNs with network segmentation and QoS tagging.

Cloud & Hybrid Architecture

We guide organizations through cloud migration and the optimization of existing infrastructure. Our architects design roadmap strategies, execute migrations across AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud, and establish ongoing governance including FinOps cost management and identity access boundaries.

Cybersecurity & Resilience

Threat surfaces evolve continuously. We protect endpoints, email, identity stores, and corporate data with proactive multi-layered defenses including vulnerability assessments, OS hardening, Endpoint Detection and Response, SIEM monitoring, phishing defenses, and incident containment forensics.

Managed Operations & NOC

For organizations seeking complete operational ownership, we deliver 24/7 proactive monitoring, server administration, and strategic IT oversight from our network operations center. Our model includes rapid helpdesk dispatch, virtualization management, cross-branch connectivity oversight, and quarterly technology roadmaps.

CCTV & Physical Surveillance

End-to-end IP video surveillance solutions engineered for comprehensive visibility, security monitoring, and regulatory compliance. Services include facility surveys, PoE IP camera installation, NVR sizing with redundant RAID storage, and encrypted remote access with motion alerts.

Backup & Disaster Recovery

Data integrity is the bedrock of business continuity. We construct resilient backup systems with air-gapped snapshots and verified restoration testing. Coverage includes automated immutable backups, secondary cloud offsite vaulting, documented recovery runbooks, and bare-metal recovery orchestration.

IT Strategy & vCIO Advisory

Technology should advance business objectives. Our advisory practice provides executive-level strategic leadership, digital transformation roadmapping, infrastructure audits, vendor evaluation and RFP drafting, IT policy creation, and multi-year budget alignment.
